Monster Hunter Outlanders魔物獵人：旅人

Hunting, parties, and getting started狩獵、組隊與入門

A practical reading of what official launch materials actually say about how you hunt, who you hunt with, and what they do not specify. Editorial notes are marked separately from those facts.這裡只整理官方上市資料真正寫過的狩獵方式、可和誰一起狩獵，以及它們沒有寫清的部分。編輯建議和這些事實分開標示。

Checked 10 November 2026 (Hong Kong time, UTC+8). English text below is quoted or closely paraphrased from the cited official page. Traditional Chinese is the official zh-tw locale string when one was published there; otherwise it is marked as a provisional translation and is not an official Chinese source.查核日期：2026年11月10日（香港時間，UTC+8）。下文英文依所引官方頁面撰寫。繁體中文若來自官方 zh-tw 語言包會註明；沒有官方中文時標為臨時翻譯，並不是官方中文原文。

Verified from official pages官方頁面已寫明

Garena describes the game as a mobile survival hunting action game, licensed and supervised by Capcom and developed by TiMi Studio Group. Capcom’s 29 September 2026 release and Garena’s Hunting FAQ both put launch on 29 October 2026. The FAQ time is 06:00 (UTC+8). The official site lists iOS and Android, and says controller support is yes. The FAQ says in-game languages include Traditional Chinese, switched under Settings, then Language.

Garena 把遊戲描述為手機上的生存狩獵動作遊戲，由 Capcom 授權並監修，天美工作室群開發。Capcom 2026年9月29日的新聞和 Garena 的 Hunting FAQ 都把上市日放在 2026年10月29日。FAQ 寫的時間是 06:00（UTC+8）。官方網站列出 iOS 與 Android，並寫控制器支援為 yes。FAQ 說遊戲內語言包含繁體中文，可在設定裡的語言項目切換。這段中文是依這些英文官方頁做的臨時翻譯。

Traditional Chinese in this paragraph is a provisional translation where the cited page is English.若引用頁是英文，本段繁體中文是臨時翻譯。

Garena’s July announcement says mobile controls were refined so a combo can be performed with one button, while keeping series-style action. Google Play says you can explore alone or form a 4-player hunting squad. Garena says real-time parties of up to four split roles, support each other, and combine co-op skills. The official site calls that system Co-Op Skills (共鬥技 in the zh-tw pack) and says coordination comes from how you use them together. Garena also names fixed-team and adventure-guild social systems, without publishing their rules.

Garena 7月的公告說，手機操作經過調整，一套連段可以用一個按鈕打出，同時保留系列的動作感。Google Play 說可以自己探索，或組成 4 人狩獵小隊。Garena 說最多 4 人的即時隊伍會分工、互相支援，並組合共鬥技能。官方網站稱這套系統為 Co-Op Skills，zh-tw 語言包寫「共鬥技」，並說默契來自操作上的配合。Garena 也點名固定隊伍和冒險公會這類社交系統，但沒有公布規則。除共鬥技的官方譯名外，這段其餘中文是臨時翻譯。

Traditional Chinese in this paragraph is a provisional translation where the cited page is English.若引用頁是英文，本段繁體中文是臨時翻譯。

The July announcement says you gather materials and craft your own equipment. It does not list recipes, weapon trees, or drop rates. The monster section of this guide already excludes weaknesses for the same reason: the public pages checked do not publish them.

7月的公告說玩家會採集素材並打造自己的裝備。它沒有配方、武器派生樹或掉落率。本手冊的魔物篇同樣不列弱點，原因一樣：查核過的公開頁面沒有公布這些表。這段中文是臨時翻譯。

Traditional Chinese in this paragraph is a provisional translation where the cited page is English.若引用頁是英文，本段繁體中文是臨時翻譯。

Garena’s 28 October 2026 FAQ says the launch servers are Korea, Asia Pacific, MidEast Africa, and Latam, on iOS and Android. Server data is separate and cannot be transferred. Cross-server parties, friend requests, and joining the same squad are not supported; friends need characters on the same server. Daily refresh is 05:00:00 server time. Recommended devices in that FAQ: iOS 17 or later, iPhone 13 Pro or later, 6 GB RAM or more; Android 12 or later, Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 or Dimensity 9200 or later, 8 GB RAM or more. Minimum in that FAQ: iOS 15 or later, iPhone 8 Plus or later, 3 GB RAM or more; Android 9 or later, Snapdragon 835 or Dimensity 920 or later, 4 GB RAM or more. The official site’s own recommended line is shorter: iOS 17 or later or Android 12 or later, and 6 GB RAM or more. Where the two differ, the FAQ is the more detailed launch note and the site line is the shorter one.

Garena 2026年10月28日的 FAQ 說，上市伺服器是韓國、亞太、中東非洲和拉丁美洲，平台是 iOS 與 Android。各伺服器資料獨立，不能轉移。不能跨伺服器組隊、加好友或加入同一小隊；和朋友玩需要在同一台伺服器建角色。每日刷新是伺服器時間 05:00:00。該 FAQ 的建議裝置：iOS 17 或以上、iPhone 13 Pro 或以上、記憶體 6 GB 或以上；Android 12 或以上、驍龍 8 Gen 2 或天璣 9200 或以上、記憶體 8 GB 或以上。FAQ 的最低需求：iOS 15 或以上、iPhone 8 Plus 或以上、記憶體 3 GB 或以上；Android 9 或以上、驍龍 835 或天璣 920 或以上、記憶體 4 GB 或以上。官方網站自己的建議行比較短：iOS 17 或以上，或 Android 12 或以上，記憶體 6 GB 或以上。兩處不一致時，FAQ 是較詳細的上市說明，網站是較短的那一行。這段中文是臨時翻譯。

Editorial notes, not game rules編輯建議，不是遊戲規則

Editorial note. This is not an official instruction and adds no unpublished game facts.編輯說明。這不是官方指示，也不補充未公布的遊戲事實。

Start from a featured Adventurer, not from a loose weapon. Each published profile is one person with one weapon. Assault (進擊型), Support (支援型), and Disruptor (控場型) are the official class names. They describe the published role. They are not a tier list, and this guide has no damage numbers to rank them.

從官方已介紹的冒險家選起，而不是先假設武器可以任意配給任何人。每一則公開介紹都是一個人搭配一種武器。進擊型、支援型、控場型是官方定位名稱，用來說明公開的角色分工，不是強度排行。本手冊沒有傷害數字可以替他們排名。

如果中文行寫著官方繁體中文名不在 zh-tw 語言包裡，請用旁邊的英文名。那個英文名就是官方網站上的名字。不要把空位或這句說明當成譯名。

For a first hunt, the sourced shape is: learn the one published blurb for your Adventurer, watch what the monster does, and use a party of up to four on the same server if you want the co-op skills the announcements describe. Anything finer than that — exact buttons beyond the “one button combo” line, item recipes, or region routes — was not in the sources checked on 10 November 2026.

若只按已查到的資料準備第一場狩獵：先讀你這位冒險家那則公開簡介，觀察魔物在做什麼，想用公告裡的共鬥技就在同一台伺服器組最多 4 人。比這更細的內容，例如「一鍵連段」之外的按鍵、道具配方或區域路線，都不在 2026年11月10日查核的來源裡。
