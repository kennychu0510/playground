Monster Hunter Outlanders魔物獵人：旅人

Adventurers and Buddies冒險家與隨從

The official site presents original Adventurers, each with one class and one weapon, plus three Buddies. The same zh-tw locale says more Adventurers are still to come, so this is the featured roster checked on 10 November 2026, not a promise that the game will never add anyone.官方網站列出原創冒險家，每位有一個定位和一種武器，另外有三種隨從。同一份 zh-tw 語言包寫著還會有更多冒險家，因此這裡是 2026年11月10日查核到的公開名單，不是「遊戲以後不會再加人」。

Checked 10 November 2026 (Hong Kong time, UTC+8). English text below is quoted or closely paraphrased from the cited official page. Traditional Chinese is the official zh-tw locale string when one was published there; otherwise it is marked as a provisional translation and is not an official Chinese source.查核日期：2026年11月10日（香港時間，UTC+8）。下文英文依所引官方頁面撰寫。繁體中文若來自官方 zh-tw 語言包會註明；沒有官方中文時標為臨時翻譯，並不是官方中文原文。

Midori

米多麗

Assault進擊型Long Sword太刀

Wields a long sword hidden in her shamisen, evading attacks with graceful movements. Some skills build her Spirit Gauge faster.

使用藏在三味線裡的特製太刀，以輕盈的優雅姿態回避攻擊。部分招式可快速累積練氣計量表，提升練氣計量表階段。

Luka

路加

Assault進擊型Bow弓

Wields a lightning-wreathed bow, darting across the battlefield with unmatched mobility. Some skills power up the Thunder Coating on the arrows on precise casts. Arrows with Thunder Coating unleash crackling arcs that pierce clean through.

使用電流纏繞的長弓，具有靈活位移的高機動性。部分招式抓準時機出招可累積電流瓶能量，使用能量射出的電弧可造成貫通傷害。

Buddies隨從

Garena’s 24 July 2026 announcement says every Adventurer is accompanied by a Buddy, and that nurturing can make them more dependable. It does not publish growth numbers. The three Buddies below are the ones named on the official site.Garena 2026年7月24日的英文公告說，每位冒險家都有隨從同行，培養之後可以變得更可靠。公告沒有公布成長數值。下面三種是官方網站點名的隨從。

Rutaco

露塔可

Buddy隨從Disruptor控場型

Buddies native to Aesoland, skilled at using tools and Radiantite to build.

本作登場的全新隨從，生活在埃索島上，擅長使用工具和融石技藝進行建造。

The official zh-tw label is 隨從露塔可.

官方 zh-tw 語言包的標籤是「隨從露塔可」。
