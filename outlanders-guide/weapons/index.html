Monster Hunter Outlanders魔物獵人：旅人

Weapons named in official materials官方資料點名的武器

The official site says these are classic series weapons with original skills, and that you control how skills come out. Google Play’s listing names eight classic weapon types and says more weapons will join in later updates. It also says each weapon forms a unique combination with Adventurers and adds follow-up moves. This page only repeats move details that an official Adventurer blurb actually states. It is not a full move list, and it does not copy move lists from World, Wilds, or Now.官方網站說這些是系列經典武器，加上原創技能，出招由玩家掌控。Google Play 的商店說明點名八種經典武器，並說之後的更新還會加入更多武器；也說每種武器會和冒險家組成獨特搭配，並有追加的追擊招式。本頁只複述官方冒險家簡介裡真正寫出的招式內容，不是完整招式表，也不從《世界》《Wilds》或 Now 抄動作表。

Long Sword

太刀

Midori’s official blurb names a Spirit Gauge. Ryunosuke’s official blurb names stance eating, a Well-fed state, and Spirit Blade attacks. Ryunosuke has no official Traditional Chinese name in the zh-tw pack checked on 10 November 2026.

米多麗的官方簡介寫到練氣計量表。Ryunosuke 的官方英文簡介寫到在特定姿勢進食、Well-fed 狀態，以及 Spirit Blade。Ryunosuke 在本次查核的 zh-tw 語言包沒有官方繁體中文名。

Dual Blades

雙劍

Raya, Keres, and Haze are the featured Dual Blades Adventurers. Raya’s official text names stamina, a Demon Gauge, and demon mode. Keres’s official text names enhanced follow-up slashes. Haze’s English text names Archdemon Mode, a parry, the Demon Gauge, and Crimson Fang Slash. Haze has no official Traditional Chinese profile in the pack checked.

官方列出的雙劍冒險家是拉雅、克蕾絲和 Haze。拉雅的官方中文寫到耐力、鬼人計量表和鬼人化。克蕾絲的官方中文寫到派生強化連斬。Haze 的英文簡介寫到 Archdemon Mode、招架、Demon Gauge 和 Crimson Fang Slash。Haze 在本次查核的語言包沒有官方繁體中文介紹。

Great Sword

大劍

Pyro and Luxheart are the featured Great Sword Adventurers. Pyro’s official text says enduring attacks powers a burst hit. Luxheart’s English text names Heat, stance changes, and a firework-like release of stored energy. Luxheart has no official Traditional Chinese profile in the pack checked.

官方列出的大劍冒險家是炎囂和 Luxheart。炎囂的官方中文寫以承受傷害來強化招式，再打出爆發性的一擊。Luxheart 的英文簡介寫到以 Heat 切換姿勢，並把能量一次放出。Luxheart 在本次查核的語言包沒有官方繁體中文介紹。

Lance

長槍

Arkaya and Ouyang Varen are the featured Lance Adventurers. Arkaya’s official text names Resolve and a hit that spends all of it while granting a large shield. Ouyang Varen’s official text names a shield deflect, Impetus in English, and 鋒銳層數 in the zh-tw blurb.

官方列出的長槍冒險家是阿坎亞和歐陽拓。阿坎亞的官方中文寫到鬥志，以及耗盡鬥志的一擊和高防護。歐陽拓的官方中文寫到盾牌招架和鋒銳層數；英文簡介把對應機制稱為 Impetus。

Charge Blade

官方繁體中文武器名未收錄

Google Play’s listing, checked 10 November 2026, names Charge Blade as one of eight classic weapon types and says more weapons will be added in future updates. The official website’s 13 featured Adventurers do not include a Charge Blade user, and the zh-tw locale checked the same day has no Charge Blade string. No move description is given here because none was published in those sources.

2026年11月10日查核的 Google Play 說明把 Charge Blade 列為八種經典武器之一，並說之後還會加入更多武器。同一天的官方網站 13 位冒險家介紹裡，沒有人使用 Charge Blade；zh-tw 語言包也沒有這個武器的條目。這裡不寫招式，因為那些來源沒有公布招式。
