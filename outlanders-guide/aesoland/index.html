Aesoland and exploration埃索島與探索

Official copy names one hunting island, Aesoland, and describes exploration, weather, and building. It does not publish a labelled region map. This section stops where those sources stop.官方文案點名一座狩獵之島「埃索島」，並描述探索、天氣和建造。沒有公布帶地名的區域地圖。本節就停在這些來源寫到的地方。

Checked 10 November 2026 (Hong Kong time, UTC+8). English text below is quoted or closely paraphrased from the cited official page. Traditional Chinese is the official zh-tw locale string when one was published there; otherwise it is marked as a provisional translation and is not an official Chinese source.查核日期：2026年11月10日（香港時間，UTC+8）。下文英文依所引官方頁面撰寫。繁體中文若來自官方 zh-tw 語言包會註明；沒有官方中文時標為臨時翻譯，並不是官方中文原文。

What is named已經點名的內容

The official zh-tw locale calls the island 埃索島. English pages call it Aesoland. Garena’s 24 July 2026 announcement says players set foot in Aesoland, “a land divided into multiple regions based on terrain and weather conditions.” The official site’s feature lines, checked 10 November 2026, say you gather resources and build facilities, open a freely explored adventure, meet changing weather and terrain, and experience a vast hunting world.

官方 zh-tw 語言包把這座島稱為埃索島，英文頁稱為 Aesoland。Garena 2026年7月24日的英文公告說，玩家踏上 Aesoland，那是「依地形和天氣分成多個區域的土地」。2026年11月10日查核的官方網站特色文案則說：採集資源、建造設施、開啟自由探索、迎接多變的天氣與地貌，並體驗遼闊的狩獵世界。

Traditional Chinese in this paragraph is a provisional translation where the cited page is English.若引用頁是英文，本段繁體中文是臨時翻譯。

The same Garena article names three field tools from the building system: launchers, ziplines, and gliding facilities. It says they let you explore each region from more than one angle, and that you can find special items and clues tied to assignments. It also says you can watch monsters hunt, taunt, and interact, and use that while hunting. No material costs, build limits, or assignment list were included.

同一篇 Garena 英文公告點名建造系統的三種野外工具：發射器（launchers）、滑索（ziplines）和滑翔設施（gliding facilities）。公告說這些工具讓人從不同角度探索每個區域，也能找到特殊物品和與任務有關的線索；探索時可以觀察魔物狩獵、挑釁和互相影響，並把觀察用在狩獵上。公告沒有材料消耗、建造上限或任務清單。這段中文是臨時翻譯。

Traditional Chinese in this paragraph is a provisional translation where the cited page is English.若引用頁是英文，本段繁體中文是臨時翻譯。

What was not published這次沒有公布的內容

The official site, its zh-tw locale, and the Garena articles checked on 10 November 2026 do not name the individual regions, show a map, or give coordinates. This guide does not invent region names or a map. There is also no published weakness chart or gathering drop table in those sources.

2026年11月10日查核的官方網站、zh-tw 語言包和 Garena 文章，都沒有寫出各區域的名字，也沒有地圖或座標。本手冊不編造區域名或地圖。這些來源同樣沒有弱點表或採集掉落表。
